## Project Highlights

- Student reports are grouped into structured investigation cases.
- Investigation findings follow a controlled taxonomy.
- Prompt injection attempts are detected and isolated.
- Proposed corrections include supporting reasoning and before-and-after comparisons.
- Faculty retain control over sensitive academic corrections, particularly answer-key changes.
- The dashboard displays real records retrieved from MongoDB Atlas.
- The application uses React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB.
